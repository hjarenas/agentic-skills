# v1.6.0

## 2026-08-20 — orchestrator bounded wait and lost-report recovery

Orchestrators gained a written contract for a worker report that never arrives, replacing an
emergent unbounded busy-wait: the wait is bounded, and at the cap completion is decided from
observed artifacts against a dispatch-time baseline rather than inferred from a missing message.
A stopped child is resumed from its own transcript by whoever dispatched it, and a report that
surfaces in the wrong conversation is relayed to the parent blocked on it — both written as normal
paths, because lost reports proved common rather than exceptional. The serialized merge slot, a
deadlock by construction when a merge/cleanup worker went silent, is now recoverable through a
read-only four-observation audit whose identity checks (feature `HEAD` is a merge commit, that
phase's recorded expected tip is one of its parents, the remote ref equals that exact `HEAD`) stop
the wrong phase's merge from releasing the slot. `discovery` and `planner` gained completion tags,
framed as report integrity rather than liveness. Two pre-existing contradictions were resolved:
`TRIP-3-release`'s nesting (nested under `TRIP-auto`, flat for a standalone `TRIP-2-implement`
run) and `TRIP-auto`'s user-interaction count.
